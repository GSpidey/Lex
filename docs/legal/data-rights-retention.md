# Data Rights, Retention and Deletion

Golden Epic Limited reviews access, correction, export, deletion, restriction and consent-withdrawal requests through account privacy controls and [PRIVACY_CONTACT]. We use proportionate identity checks, request clarification when needed and explain the result or lawful reason for refusal. Do not submit passwords or OTPs as identity evidence.

Account deletion is separate from uninstalling the app or cancelling a legal engagement. Where records must be retained for a specific legal obligation, claim, professional duty or active dispute, we identify the retained category and reason, restrict use and review the hold. A deletion request must not silently destroy another party's records or a lawyer's legally required file.

The retention schedule must specify purpose, lawful basis, trigger, period and deletion method for accounts, OTPs, sessions, authority evidence, credentials, matters, voice, transcripts, payments, AI records, audit and legal acceptances. [APPROVED_RETENTION_SCHEDULE] remains required before publication; this draft does not assert that unimplemented deletion jobs already operate. Provider retention and encrypted-backup expiry must be covered as well as the active database.

Corrections preserve provenance where necessary rather than rewriting an audit trail. Withdrawn optional consent stops future optional processing and creates a history event. On approved deletion, remove or irreversibly anonymize eligible data, record a minimal deletion marker and prevent restored backups from resurrecting deleted accounts. Exports must be identity-checked, scoped and delivered through expiring authenticated access. Rights under applicable data-protection law remain available regardless of these internal procedures.
