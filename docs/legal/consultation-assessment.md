# Consultation and Assessment Terms

The service comprises the scope shown before payment: scheduled secure text consultation, relevant asynchronous messages or voice notes, review of submitted material and a lawyer-written assessment. It does not include native voice/video calling, representation, negotiation, filing or court attendance unless separately agreed. The identified lawyer provides the professional service; Golden Epic Limited coordinates platform access.

The service record must show the fee, currency, lawyer, timing, required information and exclusions. The default test setting of NGN 10,000 and 48 hours is not an approved public tariff. The delivery timer begins only after verified payment and complete required information. A lawyer's request for missing information must explain what is needed and record any justified pause; time may not be paused secretly.

Attend the agreed consultation or use the rescheduling route promptly. Provide truthful facts and identify uncertainties. Correct transcripts and summaries before relying on them. The lawyer may explain that a question needs a different specialist, further evidence or a separately scoped engagement. The assessment should state the reviewed materials, assumptions, limitations and recommended next steps. No particular result is promised.

If the lawyer cannot provide the purchased service, you may choose eligible reassignment or the applicable refund. Duplicate charges and non-delivery are handled under the Payments Policy. A missed appointment or cancellation is assessed against the disclosed service terms and work actually performed; no undisclosed automatic forfeiture applies.
