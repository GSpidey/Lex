# Electronic Communications

Lex offers transactional communication by supported in-app messages, WhatsApp, email and device notifications. Choose the channels you can access securely. Security and service notices are distinguished from optional marketing; accepting a legal engagement does not subscribe you to advertising.

WhatsApp and email involve external delivery providers and may display previews on shared devices. Prefer assessment-ready links over sending sensitive advice in notification bodies. Verify the destination before linking a channel. Never share OTPs or approve a sign-in you did not initiate. Delivery or read status does not prove that you understood or accepted a contract.

You can change preferences, withdraw optional permissions and use STOP for supported WhatsApp opt-out. A security, payment or legal notice may still need another available lawful channel; the consequences of losing access will be explained. Push permission is requested in context and can be changed through device settings. Denying push does not cancel your account.

Electronic legal approval requires an identified version and an express authenticated action. Neither opening a link nor receiving an email counts as a signature. Keep copies of documents and receipts. Tell [SUPPORT_CONTACT] when a destination is compromised or no longer yours. The Privacy Notice explains processing and retention of delivery records.
