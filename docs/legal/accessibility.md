# Accessibility Statement

Lex aims to make legal access usable with assistive technologies, keyboard navigation, readable contrast and adjustable text. Intake should retain answers when a section is reopened, show errors near the affected field and provide a supported alternative to optional voice or camera input. Transcripts and correction controls assist users who cannot rely on audio.

Report a barrier through [ACCESSIBILITY_CONTACT], describing the screen, device or assistive technology and the task you were attempting. Do not include unnecessary legal facts in the accessibility report. We will work with you on an alternative route and assess a correction. We do not claim a formal WCAG conformance level or certification without a supporting audit.

Language support does not guarantee that automated translation is correct. Important legal and consent text requires suitable review, and users may ask for clarification before acceptance. Emergency or safeguarding matters should not depend solely on an inaccessible control; available human support should explain another safe route without promising emergency service.
