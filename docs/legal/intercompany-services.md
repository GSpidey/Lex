# Intercompany Technology and Publishing Agreement — Review Draft

## Parties and purpose
Golden Epic Limited, Nigerian RC 1538879, and GoldenEpic Solutions Inc., Canadian corporation 1641963-1, propose the following allocation for Lex. This is a private draft, not an executed agreement or a public policy. Each signatory must establish authority for the company represented. A co-founder legal review is not automatically execution on behalf of both companies.

Golden Epic Limited operates the Nigerian marketplace, contracts with Nigerian platform customers, coordinates professional onboarding, handles platform complaints and proposes the Nigerian merchant relationship. Independent lawyers provide legal services under their engagements. GoldenEpic Solutions Inc. supplies software development, maintenance, hosting administration and store publishing where permitted by the store and documented rights.
