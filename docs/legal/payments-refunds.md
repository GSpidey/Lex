# Payments, Cancellations, Refunds and Settlement

Golden Epic Limited is the proposed Nigerian platform merchant. The checkout must identify the merchant, professional service provider, currency, total price, included taxes or expenses and any client-borne fee before confirmation. The responsible lawyer clears conflicts before the initial payment is requested. Representation invoices require their own approved scope and payment.

Payment is successful only after server-side provider verification. A redirect, screenshot or pending debit alone is not confirmation. If money appears debited but the matter remains unpaid, retain the reference and request reconciliation instead of repeatedly paying. Confirmed duplicate collections must be investigated and the duplicate amount returned where due.

You may request cancellation before delivery. Where no service has been provided, or the assigned lawyer cannot deliver, request reassignment or a full refund of the affected purchased service. For partially completed representation or assessment work, any proposed deduction must identify the agreed, actually performed work and lawful basis; it is not an automatic blanket non-refundable fee. Mandatory consumer rights take priority. A reassignment does not authorize a second charge for the same undelivered service.

Refund requests are reviewed with the payment and work record. Approved refunds are initiated through the original provider where possible and tracked to completion; provider and bank processing times vary. We will communicate status rather than guarantee a banking settlement date. Chargebacks may proceed through your bank; cooperate with evidence requests and avoid double recovery. A dispute does not authorize retaliation or destruction of records.
