# Acceptable Use

Use Lex for lawful enquiries and authorized professional work. Do not impersonate clients, guardians, organizations or lawyers; fabricate credentials; alter evidence deceptively; submit malware; harass or threaten others; exploit a child; infringe rights; or attempt unauthorized access, payment bypass or security interference. Do not place unrelated credentials, card secrets or excessive third-party information in a matter.

Good-faith reporting, criticism, complaints and lawful evidence submission are not prohibited merely because they concern wrongdoing. Material that is relevant to a legal issue may be distressing or disputed; explain its context and avoid unnecessary copies. Reporting an allegation does not establish its truth.

Lex may investigate misuse, preserve relevant evidence and restrict the affected operation or account proportionately. Where lawful and safe, we explain the reason and provide a review route. Immediate action may be necessary to protect a person, secure accounts or comply with law. We do not automatically suspend unrelated services because one upload or feature fails.

Report abuse through [COMPLAINTS_CONTACT] and urgent safeguarding concerns through [SAFEGUARDING_CONTACT]. Do not use this reporting channel as an emergency response service. Professional misconduct concerns may also be directed to the appropriate professional body. Enforcement must respect confidentiality, lawful retention and the rights of affected parties.
