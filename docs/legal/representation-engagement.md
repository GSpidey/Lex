# Representation Engagement Terms

An initial consultation does not authorize ongoing representation. Representation begins only through a separate proposal identifying the client, responsible lawyer or firm, scope, excluded work, fee basis, expenses, milestones, client responsibilities and cancellation provisions, followed by acceptance and required conflicts confirmation. Lex is not a party providing professional representation merely because it hosts the workflow.

The lawyer must confirm applicable deadlines and explain the agreed reporting process. Platform reminders assist coordination; they do not calculate a legal deadline, extend time or prove that a filing occurred. The client should promptly communicate notices and developments. Only an expressly authorized practitioner may carry out regulated professional acts.

Each invoice identifies the engagement and work or milestone charged, with a preserved allocation and fee snapshot. Approval of a proposal does not authorize unlimited expenses, automatic card charges or unagreed additional work. A material scope or fee change requires a documented variation accepted by the affected parties.

Either party may seek termination subject to professional duties, applicable law and the agreed terms. Record work completed, unearned sums, outstanding expenses, file access, handover and any deadline risk. Do not delete evidence or communications to resolve a dispute. The lawyer remains responsible for lawful withdrawal and client protection. Refunds, disputed amounts and chargebacks follow the applicable invoice and Payments Policy without preventing professional or court remedies.
