# Cross-Border Processing and Service Providers

Golden Epic Limited uses technology and service providers for defined operational purposes. GoldenEpic Solutions Inc. is the proposed technology provider, subject to executed terms. Providers may act as processors or independent controllers for particular activities; their role must be recorded rather than assumed from a vendor name.

The current integration inventory includes Cloudflare for staging compute, data and private objects; Meta for WhatsApp delivery; OpenAI for consented AI and transcription; Paystack and Flutterwave for payments; Postmark for email; and Expo with underlying Apple/Google services for push. An implemented integration is not necessarily active.
