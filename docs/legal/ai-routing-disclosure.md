# AI Assistance and Automated Routing

Lex can use AI to organize an enquiry, suggest practice areas and missing questions, transcribe voice, translate intake confirmations, summarize facts and assist lawyer drafting. It does not replace a lawyer, provide an independently approved legal opinion, predict outcomes, calculate court deadlines or choose the final lawyer for you.

Before sending matter information to an AI provider, Lex explains the purpose and requests the applicable permission. You can decline optional AI and use available structured intake. Do not include passwords, verification codes, payment secrets or irrelevant personal records. Only information needed for the stated task should be transmitted. Relevant providers and international processing are described in the Subprocessors and Privacy Notices.

AI can misunderstand accents, language, chronology, names and legal categories. Inspect and correct the transcript, proposed classification and English record before confirming them. Confirmation means the record reflects what you told us; it does not mean a lawyer has approved a conclusion. English is the authoritative professional record, with supported-language explanations for client confirmation.

Urgency, conflicting facts, safeguarding concerns and uncertain output require human review. Ask for a human or challenge routing through the matter controls. A challenge must not be treated as acceptance of the contested result. AI cannot mark an assessment lawyer-reviewed; only the responsible lawyer can attest and deliver it.
