# Minors, Guardians, Safeguarding and Organizations

A person under 18 may begin with a minimal account. Detailed legal facts, documents, lawyer sharing and payment require the relevant verified guardian authority and lawful processing conditions. Guardian verification uses proportionate identity and relationship evidence and recorded consent; possessing a phone or payment card alone does not prove guardianship.

If a guardian may be implicated in abuse, a conflict or an unsafe disclosure, use the safeguarding route. Restricted staff assess a lawful protective response; ordinary guardian access must not automatically expose the enquiry. Exceptions based on vital interests, court authority or other legal grounds require recorded human decisions. Lex does not promise emergency protection or immediate response. Seek appropriate local emergency assistance for immediate danger.

An organization representative must identify the entity, their role and their authority to instruct. Operations verifies the appropriate registration and authority evidence before sharing, matching or payment. An employee is not automatically authorized to bind an organization. Membership removal must address future access without erasing valid records or changing the identity of the client.

The responsible lawyer separately determines whom they represent and whether conflicts or professional restrictions prevent acting. The platform's account approval is not a court determination of guardianship, capacity or authority. False authority, unsafe access and mistaken information can be reported through [SAFEGUARDING_CONTACT] or the restricted in-app review route. Only necessary reviewers should see identity and safeguarding evidence.
