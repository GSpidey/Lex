# Cookie Notice and Preferences

Lex uses essential browser storage for authenticated sessions, security and your consent choices. Essential storage is used only for those purposes. The mobile application also uses secure device storage for credentials and draft recovery; browser cookie preferences do not remove a mobile account.

Optional analytics, personalization and marketing storage must remain off until you choose the relevant purpose. Rejecting optional storage does not prevent access to legal services. You can change or withdraw your choice through Cookie Preferences; withdrawal stops future optional processing and initiates removal of the optional identifiers controlled by Lex. Providers may retain prior records where their disclosed terms and applicable law permit.

The deployment must maintain a technology register naming each cookie or local-storage item, its owner, purpose, duration and recipients. No optional technology may be activated solely because this notice lists a category. The initial release uses no advertising trackers. Essential account tokens expire or are revoked through account/session controls; clearing browser storage signs you out but does not delete server records.
