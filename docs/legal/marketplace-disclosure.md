# Marketplace and Legal Services Disclosure

Golden Epic Limited operates Lex as a technology and service-coordination platform. Independent verified lawyers or identified law firms provide legal advice. Professional verification checks eligibility at a recorded time; it is not a guarantee of an outcome or a substitute for the lawyer's continuing duties.

Matching uses practice area, jurisdiction, language, current verification, availability, workload and relevant urgency capability. Lawyers cannot purchase ranking. Clients choose from eligible results where coverage exists. Where no eligible lawyer is available, operations-assisted matching occurs before payment. Urgent and safeguarding requests require restricted human review. Lex is not an emergency response service.

The chosen lawyer must clear conflicts before payment for the initial service. Provide accurate party information, but avoid detailed confidential disclosures beyond what the intake requests before conflict clearance. You may decline a suggested match, correct classification or ask for human review. A matching result or AI summary does not itself establish representation.

Before commitment, the service record identifies the lawyer, scope, price, service timing and applicable terms. Advice is delivered only through lawyer attestation. Additional representation, negotiations, filings or court work require a separate engagement; they are not included simply because the same matter remains visible in Lex.
