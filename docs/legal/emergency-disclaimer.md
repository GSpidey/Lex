# Emergencies and Outcomes

Lex is not an emergency service. It cannot promise immediate lawyer availability, police intervention, protection, medical response, bail, an injunction or any court result. If there is immediate danger, seek appropriate local emergency assistance or a trusted safe support channel. Do not wait for an AI reply or scheduled consultation when delay may endanger someone.

Urgency flags send relevant requests to restricted human review. They do not guarantee a response time, establish representation or suspend legal deadlines. Avoid disclosing an unsafe enquiry to a person who may cause harm; use the restricted safeguarding route where applicable.

An assessment describes a lawyer's judgment on the information and scope available. Outcomes depend on facts, evidence, other parties and decision-makers. No platform rating, AI output, marketing statement or fee guarantees success. Ask the responsible lawyer to clarify deadlines, immediate protective options, limitations and any further engagement needed. Lex does not calculate legal deadlines or file documents on your behalf.
