# Complaints and Dispute Resolution

Submit a complaint through the account support controls or [COMPLAINTS_CONTACT]. Include a matter or payment reference, the issue, relevant dates and the outcome you seek. Do not send verification codes or unnecessary sensitive documents in ordinary email. Assistance must remain available for accessibility barriers and account-access problems.

We record receipt, classify the concern, identify the responsible team and explain next steps. Safety and account-compromise concerns receive priority assessment. The reviewer should not be the person whose conduct is being challenged where an independent reviewer is available. We may request proportionate evidence and give the other affected party an opportunity to respond where lawful and safe.

The outcome should explain findings, any correction, refund, reassignment or other remedy, and how to request further review. A provider settlement delay is tracked separately from a complaint decision. The reviewed operational schedule will publish acknowledgement and response targets: [COMPLAINT_RESPONSE_SCHEDULE]. We will explain material delays rather than imply that a request has been resolved.

Platform complaints, payment disputes and professional-conduct concerns may have different responsible bodies. You retain the right to contact your bank, relevant consumer or privacy regulator, professional body or court. No mandatory arbitration waiver is imposed by this draft. Informal resolution is voluntary and does not remove non-waivable rights or extend legal deadlines.
