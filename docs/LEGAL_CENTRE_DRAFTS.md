## Platform Terms and Conditions

Identify the contracting entity, eligibility, account security, accurate-information duties, acceptable use, suspension, intellectual property, third-party providers, platform limits, complaints, changes, notices and termination. Lex is a managed legal-access technology platform, not a law firm or emergency service. A lawyer-client relationship arises only under the applicable lawyer engagement, not by browsing, using AI intake or receiving a shortlist. No outcome, lawyer availability or uninterrupted service is guaranteed.

## Privacy Notice

Describe the controller, data categories, purposes, legal bases, recipients, processors, cross-border transfers, retention, security, children’s safeguards, automated routing, human review, rights requests and complaint channels. Cover identity and contact information, credentials, matter facts, parties, documents, voice, transcripts, messages, sessions, consent, payments and audits. Credentials and payment secrets are excluded from AI inputs; only minimized matter content may be processed for the recorded purpose.

## Web Cookie Notice and Preferences

Essential storage may support authentication, security and consent records. Analytics, preference and marketing categories remain off until opt-in and provider approval. List each non-essential technology, owner, purpose, duration and recipient before activation. Withdrawal must be as easy as consent.

## Mobile EULA

Grant a limited, revocable licence for lawful use. Prohibit credential sharing, harmful uploads and interference. Address app updates, app-store terms, open-source notices, device security, support, suspension and termination. Deleting the app does not itself delete the account or retained records.

## Client Marketplace and Legal-Services Disclosure

Explain approval-controlled matching, versioned services, conflict checks before payment, independent lawyer responsibility and non-purchasable ranking. Lex organizes intake and communication but does not attest legal opinions or guarantee a shortlist. `matching_assistance` and urgent review occur before payment where required. Clients choose among eligible lawyers and may challenge automated routing.

## Lawyer Participation Terms

Require current standing, accurate capability and availability, conflicts compliance, confidentiality, competent service, secure use, timely communication, lawyer-only attestation and renewal evidence. Cover profiles, ranking, complaints, suspension, audits, AI drafting, payouts, refunds and termination. Production fee allocation remains disabled until professional-conduct review is recorded.

## Consultation and Assessment Terms

Define scheduled live text, secure messages, voice notes, evidence review and a written assessment; exclude native calls, court filing, representation and emergency response unless separately engaged. State when the SLA starts and pauses, client duties, reassignment/refund remedies and lawyer attestation. AI cannot deliver or label work lawyer-reviewed.

## Representation Engagement Terms

Require a separate proposal stating lawyer or firm, scope, exclusions, fee basis, milestones, expenses, cancellation and responsibilities, followed by conflict reconfirmation. Lex tracks tasks, lawyer-confirmed deadlines, documents, invoices and messages but does not calculate deadlines, appear, file, negotiate or represent the client.

## Payments, Cancellations, Refunds and Settlement Policy

Identify provider, currency, amount, fee bearer, allocation snapshot and verification rule. Redirect screens do not prove payment. Explain failed settlements, full and partial refunds, duplicate prevention, chargebacks, disputes, reassignment and invoice payments. Lex does not promise escrow. Sandbox 20/80 allocation is not a production commitment.

## AI-Assisted Intake and Automated-Routing Disclosure

Explain classification, transcription, extraction, summarization, translation, missing-information prompts, routing support and lawyer drafting. Prohibit autonomous advice, outcome prediction, deadline calculation, final lawyer selection and attestation. Mark AI content; let clients inspect, correct, reject and confirm it. Urgent, safeguarding, conflicting, unsupported and low-confidence results require human review.

## Electronic Communications Consent

Record separate WhatsApp, email and push preferences. Explain transactional versus optional messages, templates, delivery events, provider processing, retries, opt-out commands and consequences. Security or legal notices may use another available lawful channel. Personal phone numbers remain hidden between client and lawyer.

## Minor, Guardian, Safeguarding and Organization Terms

Allow only a minimal minor account until verified guardian authority; prohibit detailed facts, evidence sharing, matching and payment before approval. Describe identity and relationship evidence, restricted safeguarding review, suspected-abuse or guardian-conflict exceptions and least-disclosure access. Organization representatives must prove authority before lawyer sharing or payment.

## Acceptable Use Policy

Prohibit unlawful, abusive, fraudulent, infringing or malicious activity; impersonation; unauthorized practice; evidence tampering; unrelated secrets; harassment; and security or payment bypass. Permit proportionate suspension, evidence preservation and review with notice where safe and lawful.

## Complaints and Dispute-Resolution Procedure

Provide accessible intake, acknowledgement, triage, safeguarding escalation, evidence preservation, response targets, review and external-regulator information. Separate complaints about Lex, providers and independent lawyers. Do not suppress lawful reporting. Governing-law and arbitration choices remain pending.

## Copyright and Intellectual-Property Notice

Identify ownership or licensing of Lex software, brands, design, workflows and authored content. Preserve client ownership of submissions subject to a processing licence and lawyer responsibility for professional work subject to client-use rights. Do not claim ownership of public law.

## Emergency and No-Outcome-Guarantee Disclaimer

Lex is not an emergency service. Immediate danger, detention, violence, child-safety risk or medical emergency requires appropriate local resources. Operations escalation is not a guaranteed response. No lawyer, AI system or platform feature guarantees an outcome, bail, filing, settlement, deadline extension or recovery.
