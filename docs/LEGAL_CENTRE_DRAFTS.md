# Lex Legal Centre Review Pack

These are proposed, unapproved legal documents for the Nigerian Lex service. Golden Epic Limited is the proposed operator and merchant; GoldenEpic Solutions Inc. provides technology and may publish the apps subject to eligibility and documented rights. The companies are not assumed to be parent and subsidiary.

## Documents

The substantive versioned drafts are in [legal](legal/):

- Platform terms, privacy notice, cookie notice and mobile EULA.
- Marketplace disclosure, lawyer participation, consultation and assessment, representation engagement.
- Payments and refunds, AI routing, electronic communications and protected accounts.
- Acceptable use, complaints, data rights and retention, accessibility, copyright, emergencies and subprocessors.
- A private intercompany services agreement, excluded from public policy listings.

The files are the source for migration 0006. The database retains earlier versions. Once a migration has been applied, corrections must be made as new versions, never by changing the applied migration.

## Paperless review

1. Sign in to the web workspace and select your assigned Owner role.
2. Open Legal Centre → Review drafts and approvals, then verify your authenticator code.
3. Read and revise documents. A revision creates a new version; it never overwrites a reviewed document.
4. Select up to 20 latest draft versions and a different Owner with an assigned Lawyer role. Create a packet.
5. Send the invitation through the configured email sender. A queued invitation is not evidence of delivery. If email is unavailable, use Prepare private invitation link and share it only with the assigned reviewer through a trusted channel. Each fresh invitation invalidates older unclaimed links.
6. The intended recipient signs in to their own Owner account, verifies MFA and claims the expiring, single-use invitation.
7. The reviewer can comment, request changes, or expressly approve with a typed legal name. Approval binds the preserved content hashes. Opening a link is not approval.
8. The requesting Owner separately publishes approved versions after required fields are resolved. Changed content requires a new review.
9. Download the JSON review record containing versions, hashes, decisions, names and timestamps.

The interface is at /legal/review/. Effective public policies are at /legal/ without sign-in. Drafts never displace a previous effective policy. The intercompany draft is restricted to owners even after its review status changes.

Bracketed fields are tracked unresolved inputs, not facts to invent. Approving a review packet is not automatically corporate execution for both companies, regulatory approval, or permission for public release.
